## 💡 About The Website

**Kedar's Physics Engine** is a digital study notebook designed specifically for CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 senior physics students.

Physics is often perceived as intimidating due to dense algebraic formulas and abstract concepts. Standard digital textbooks and static PDFs can feel cold, monotonous, and tiring to study for hours. This website transforms the learning experience into a warm, inviting, hand-written student notebook:

* **Warm Dotted Notebook Theme**: An authentic ivory-cream canvas with a delicate dot-grid texture, spiral-bound notebook margins, and natural handwriting typography that feels like flipping through a mentor's master notebook.
* **Fluorescent Highlighter Formula Boxes**: Key equations, physical constants, and fundamental laws are highlighted in vibrant fluorescent neon shades (yellow, emerald, electric cyan, and coral) just like a student's revision notebook.
* **Strict NCERT Line-by-Line Alignment**: The core chapters contain zero unnecessary out-of-syllabus filler. Every definition, derivation, and numerical problem maps directly to the latest rationalized CBSE board curriculum.
* **Clean Competitive Separation**: Advanced JEE Main and JEE Advanced topics (such as non-inertial frames, pseudo forces, complex projectile trajectories, and rationalized proofs) are cleanly partitioned into a dedicated "Advanced Studies" section. Board students can study with total peace of mind knowing the core notes are 100% focused on their exam paper.
* **Step-Wise Scoring Transparency**: Model answers feature official CBSE step-marking breakdowns (`[½ Mark]`, `[1 Mark]`), training students on exact mathematical presentation, required justifications, and SI unit discipline.

## 📚 The 5-Tab Study System

Every chapter across both Class 11 and Class 12 is structured into five synchronized notebook tabs:

### 1. 📘 NCERT Core Notes
* First-principles derivations and line-by-line concept breakdowns with zero mathematical steps skipped.
* Hand-sketched doodle cards and color-coded sticky notes highlighting critical physical insights.
* **Subtopic Quick Navigator**: Interactive topic chips at the top of every chapter allow instant jumps to any subtopic without scrolling through dozens of pages.
* Integrated interactive physics laboratories and intuitive explanation toggles.

### 2. 💡 Solved Examples & CBSE Marking Schemes
* Foundational, conceptual, and numerical textbook problems solved step-by-step.
* Explicit CBSE marking breakdown showing how examiners award marks for:
  * Formula quotation with standard physical symbols `[½ Mark]`
  * Value substitution with correct SI conversions `[½ Mark]`
  * Final numerical evaluation with proper unit and vector notation `[1 Mark]`

### 3. 🎯 Board Exam PYQ Vault (2020–2025)
* A curated archive of **over 420 authentic CBSE board exam questions** from recent board exam papers (2020 through 2025).
* Filterable by question typology and marks weightage:
  * **1-Mark MCQs & Assertion-Reasoning**: Conceptual traps and quick tests.
  * **2-Mark Very Short Answer (VSA)**: Two-step evaluations and definitions.
  * **3-Mark Short Answer (SA)**: Standard derivations and numericals.
  * **4-Mark Case-Based Competency Questions**: Paragraph passages with multiple conceptual sub-parts.
  * **5-Mark Long Answer (LA)**: Comprehensive derivations, multi-part proofs, and full-length problems.
* Interactive mastery checklist allowing students to check off questions as they practice.

### 4. ⚠️ Examiner Traps & Speed Hacks
* **High-Frequency Traps**: Common pitfalls where students routinely lose marks (e.g., forgetting vector arrows over fields $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$, omitting SI units, confusing angle of incidence with glancing angle, or signs in lens formulas).
* **Speed Hacks & Sanity Checks**: Practical dimensional checks, limiting-case verifications ($\theta \to 0^\circ, 90^\circ$), and mnemonic shortcuts to verify answers before submitting the exam paper.

### 5. 🚀 Advanced Studies & JEE Extension
* Reserved for competitive exam aspirants (JEE Main & JEE Advanced).
* Higher-level mathematical extensions, calculus-intensive proofs, and tricky problem variations.
* Kept strictly segregated so board exam students are never overwhelmed.

## 🔬 Interactive Physics Laboratories

The website features 8 custom interactive HTML5 physics simulations with real-time parameter sliders, animated particle dynamics, and live telemetry:

1. **Projectile Motion** (*Motion in a Plane*): Adjust launch speed, launch angle, and gravity to observe real-time parabolic trajectories, maximum height $H_{\max}$, flight time $T$, and range $R$.
2. **Inclined Plane & Free Body Diagram (FBD)** (*Laws of Motion*): Vary incline angle $\theta$, mass $m$, and surface friction $\mu$ to inspect live normal force vectors, gravitational components, and resultant acceleration.
3. **Harmonic Oscillator & Energy Distribution** (*Oscillations*): Real-time mass-spring oscillation with dynamic kinetic and potential energy bar graphs proving total mechanical energy conservation ($E_k + E_p = E_{\text{total}}$).
4. **Coulomb Electrostatic Field & Force** (*Electrostatics*): Position two point charges, adjust charge magnitudes, and visualize 2D field lines with attractive vs repulsive force readouts.
5. **Ohm's Law & Drift Velocity** (*Current Electricity*): Control applied voltage and resistance to observe electron drift motion inside a conductor with live current telemetry.
6. **Lorentz Force & Cyclotron Orbit** (*Moving Charges & Magnetism*): Adjust magnetic field strength and particle velocity to watch charged particles trace circular cyclotron orbits ($r = \frac{mv}{qB}$).
7. **Snell's Law & Total Internal Reflection (TIR)** (*Ray Optics*): Alter refractive indices $n_1, n_2$ and angle of incidence to witness critical angle $\theta_c$ phenomena and total internal reflection.
8. **Bohr Atom Spectral Transitions** (*Atoms & Nuclei*): Trigger electron transitions between quantized orbits ($n=1$ to $5$) and calculate the emitted photon's exact wavelength in the electromagnetic spectrum.

* **Academic Standard Mode**: Formal, mathematically rigorous definitions and equations.
* **ELI5 Mode**: Clear, down-to-earth analogies grounded in everyday experiences (e.g., thinking of electric flux as light shining through a frosted glass box, or electric potential as water pressure in pipes).
* Switching modes triggers an encouraging visual response with celebratory confetti to reward students for seeking deep conceptual understanding.
